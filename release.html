INSTALLATION & QUICKSTART GUIDE

How to Install on Your School PC

1

Download the Verified Installer Package

Click the "Download Now" button above or in the sidebar. Save the installer file to your computer's Downloads folder or directly to a USB flash drive for easy distribution to staff room PCs.

2

Run the Setup Wizard

Double-click the downloaded setup file. Follow the quick on-screen prompts. No internet connection or third-party database servers (like MySQL or SQL Server) are needed.

3

Configure School Identity & Grading System

Launch the application. Enter your school's official name, address, motto, and upload your school crest/logo. Select your state or WAEC/NECO continuous assessment weighting (e.g., 30/70 or 40/60).

4

Import Students & Enter Subject Scores

Easily import class registers from Excel/CSV files or enter students manually. Subject teachers can input Test 1, Test 2, and Exam scores directly.

5

Generate Broadsheets & Print Termly Report Cards

Instantly compute overall averages, class positions, psychomotor domain ratings, and principal remarks. Export the entire class to single or multi-page PDF report cards ready for printing or WhatsApp distribution to parents.
