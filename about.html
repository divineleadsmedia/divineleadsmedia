WHO WE ARE & OUR MISSION

About Divine Leads Media.

We are dedicated to transforming education across Nigeria through practical technology, classroom research, equipment supplies, and learner skills.

THE COHESIVE APPROACH

A Complete, Cohesive Approach to Transforming School Education

We do not treat technology in isolation. We unite research, knowledge, software, equipment, and learner skills into one unified solution.

PILLAR 1

Digital & AI Skills for Learners

We are committed to filling the gap between the theoretical aspect of the current Nigerian educational system and the relevant skills necessary for learners to thrive in the digital economy. We organize group seminars, group tutorials, and in-person tutorials to teach Nigerian learners what they need to know, providing practical aspects of what they have been taught in school and positioning them better to thrive in a digital economy.

Practical Student Training
PILLAR 2

Empowering Educators with Digital & AI Skills

We organize conferences, webinars, and individual trainings for schools and individuals on how to leverage technology and AI tools to improve learning and be more productive. We believe that the reason why it seems there is a degradation of excellence among students is because teaching methods need a revamp that suits the complexities and dynamics of the current digital generation.

Educator Development

  • Error-free student report cards in minutes

FLAGSHIP SCHOOL SOFTWARE

DLM ResultDesk — End the Stress of Manual School Report Cards & Broadsheets

DLM ResultDesk is built specifically for Nigerian primary and secondary schools. Teachers simply enter test and exam scores once, and ResultDesk instantly computes total marks, averages, subject ranks, class positions, and prints clean student report cards with your school logo.

  • Automatic Broadsheets: Enter marks once; software computes totals, positions, and averages.
  • Print Clean Report Cards: Error-free PDF report sheets with school badges in minutes.
