FLAGSHIP SCHOOL SOFTWARE

DLM ResultDesk — End the Stress of Manual School Report Cards & Broadsheets

DLM ResultDesk is built specifically for Nigerian primary and secondary schools. Teachers simply enter test and exam scores once, and ResultDesk instantly computes total marks, averages, subject ranks, class positions, and prints clean student report cards with your school logo.

  • Automatic Broadsheets: Enter continuous assessment (CA) and exam marks once, and the software calculates totals, subject ranks, and class averages automatically.
  • Print Clean Report Cards: Generate beautiful, error-free PDF report sheets with school badges, principal remarks, and performance charts in minutes.
  • Works Completely Offline: Runs smoothly on any standard Windows laptop or desktop computer with zero internet connection needed.
  • Follows Nigerian Standards: Pre-configured for Nursery, Primary, Junior, and Senior Secondary grading rules (WAEC, NECO, and BECE scales).
